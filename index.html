🚂 Day Trips from London

Hop on a train and explore beyond the city — click any card to ask Jojo AI for tips!

🏛️ Oxford

~1 hr from Paddington · GWR trains

Home to one of the world's oldest universities. Explore the Bodleian Library, Christ Church College, Covered Market and the Ashmolean Museum.

From ~£15 return ♿ Accessible

🛁 Bath

~1.5 hrs from Paddington · GWR trains

UNESCO World Heritage city with Roman Baths, stunning Georgian architecture, the Royal Crescent, and independent boutiques.

From ~£20 return ♿ Accessible

🎓 Cambridge

~50 mins from King's Cross · LNER/Thameslink

Punt along the River Cam, visit King's College Chapel, and stroll the famous Backs — stunning gardens behind the colleges.

From ~£12 return ♿ Accessible

👑 Windsor

~30 mins from Paddington · GWR trains

Windsor Castle — the oldest occupied castle in the world and official Royal residence. Beautiful Long Walk and Great Park too.

From ~£10 return ♿ Accessible

🗿 Stonehenge

~2 hrs · Waterloo → Salisbury → shuttle bus

One of the world's most famous prehistoric monuments, over 5,000 years old. Book timed entry tickets in advance online.

From ~£25 return ♿ Accessible

🎡 Brighton

~50 mins from Victoria or London Bridge

Vibrant seaside city with a famous pier, the Royal Pavilion, quirky shops in The Lanes, street food and pebble beaches.

From ~£12 return ♿ Accessible
🎟️ Train booking tip: Book in advance on nationalrail.co.uk or thetrainline.com for the cheapest fares. A 16-25 Railcard saves 1/3 on most journeys!

🗺️ London Neighbourhoods

Every area has its own character — find yours

🎨 Shoreditch

Street art, trendy cafes, vintage shops and London's best nightlife scene. Great for creative types.

🌸 Notting Hill

Portobello Road Market, pastel-coloured houses, antiques and the famous annual Carnival every August.

🎭 Covent Garden

Street performers, boutique shops, great restaurants and the Royal Opera House. Lively all day.

🎪 South Bank

Tate Modern, London Eye, Shakespeare's Globe and the Southbank food market. Fully accessible riverside walk.

🎸 Camden

Alternative culture, Camden Market, live music venues, street food from around the world and unique fashion.

🌐 Greenwich

The Prime Meridian, Royal Observatory, Cutty Sark and Greenwich Park. Easy by DLR or Thames Clipper.
